For owners of property who live in their home, they pay the taxe foncière but not taxe habitation as well. The impôts sur revenue should be paid by everyone who resides in France more than 3 months( tourists excluded).The rate should be 10% of their net  annual revenue.  A person who only makes 1300€ per month should contribute 130€ for the government services he enjoys. A person making 100 000€ per month would pay 10 000€. In this way you have extended the tax basis for more revenue and have treated all equally with the same tax basis.
